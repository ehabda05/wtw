## Spec ambiguities found while writing these

1. **Noise budget vs. the scheduler.** In any window of 24h or less, Nudge A (at 50%), Nudge B (deadline − 2h) and the unprompted deadline lock all fall within 12h of each other. The spec doesn't say whether "collide" looks ahead or back, or what happens once a nudge has already gone out before a higher-priority lock or reminder. The fixtures use a half-open `[t, t+12h)` window and work around it: 04 puts the nudge exactly 12h before the lock, and 06 makes A and B fall due at the same moment so they merge.
2. **Prompted or unprompted?** Is the one-time intro unprompted? Is an opted-in call or DM "a reminder that a member explicitly asked for"? The hungry flow's 10-minute timers are scheduler-fired, so by the letter they are unprompted.
3. **Option spread rule vs. examples.** "Never three consecutive days when a wider spread exists" contradicts the doc's own Thu/Fri/Sat example. Ties between days with equal scores are unspecified. When a trigger has both a window and named days ("this week? thinking thu, fri or sat"), it isn't clear which wins.
4. **Veto-and-swap.** Which window regenerates the fresh options? Do earlier day-level prefs carry over? If they do, "everyone answered" could re-lock instantly. Do opening hours get re-checked for the new day?
5. **Two options on one day.** Weekday-level prefs can't tell Sat brunch from Sat 7pm. "brunch" and "late" aren't time-constraint words. A bare "4" is a date by §3 but an index once a bridge has added option 4.
6. **Rule 6 vs. rule 7.** A bare "down" counts, but "fri for me" (references plus non-cue words) and a bridge answer like "yes 8:30 works" (no option reference) are undefined. Mixed cues ("fri prob works") and "either fri or sat" (where "either" means *all*) are also unclear.
7. **Lock edge cases.** Does the "winner strands someone" bridge apply to early, threshold or forced locks? Is "Fri unless someone says sat…" final? Rule 6 counts only votes in `collecting_availability`, so how are confirm-ping answers and late votes handled after the lock?
8. **Hungry flow while the date is open.** Are date votes parsed during `collecting_place`? A "1" could mean either poll. "Open at the plan's time" has no time before a date is chosen.
9. **Food intent edges.** An addressed message with one weak word ("otto this lecture is eating me alive") triggers by rule 1, because the idiom list has "eating me up" but not "eating me alive". "Where should we eat fri" during a plan could be plan intent or food intent. `otto place: X` is unspecified.
10. **Gaps.** The send time of the day-before reminder is unspecified. "tomorrow" after midnight is unclear. Nothing says what happens when filters leave fewer than 3 places. There is no copy for an empty dump. Instant plans have no lock type. If a quiet-hours deadline moves to 9:30am, it can exceed now + 24h.
